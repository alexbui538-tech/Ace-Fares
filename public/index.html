Our fleet

Every car is cleaned and checked before each trip. Prices are per day, with miles included.

How it works

No rental counter and no line. You deal with the owner from booking to return.

  1. Choose your car and dates

    Pick a car from the fleet, set your trip dates and choose pickup or delivery. You see the full price before you pay.

  2. Book and pay online

    Checkout is handled securely by Stripe. You get a confirmation by email, and we text you to set a handoff time.

  3. Pick up and drive

    Meet us at the car or get it delivered. We each snap a few photos of the car on our phones (all sides, mileage and fuel), and you're off. Do the same when you return it.
